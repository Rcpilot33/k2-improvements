KAMP replaces the fixed front-left purge line with a purge placed near
the current print and inside the usable bed area.

Before using KAMP:
  1. Enable "Label objects" in your slicer.
  2. Use the bundled machine-start G-code template for your slicer.
  3. Confirm the template contains M109 before LINE_PURGE.

Creality Print templates:
  slicer-templates/creality-print-material-machine-start.gcode
  slicer-templates/creality-print-plate-selection-machine-start.gcode
  slicer-templates/creality-print-kamp-machine-start.gcode
  slicer-templates/creality-print-kamp-and-plate-selection-machine-start.gcode

Orca template:
  slicer-templates/orca-machine-start.gcode

The installer does not restart Klipper. After restarting the firmware,
power-cycle the printer before the next G28.
