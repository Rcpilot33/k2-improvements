Replaces the stock PR Touch probe with Cartographer scanning and touch
probing.

Before installing:
  - Mount and connect a compatible Cartographer V3 or V4 by USB.
  - Know which physical mount and offset preset you are using.
  - Stop any active print.

After installing:
  1. Select the matching mount-offset profile.
  2. Power-cycle the printer before homing.
  3. Complete Cartographer calibration.

Probe firmware flashing is a separate, optional menu action.
